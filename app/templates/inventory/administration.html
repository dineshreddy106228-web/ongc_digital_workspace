Assets and their SAP plant codes

SAP keeps a plant code per legacy asset, so a merger leaves two codes reporting into one place — N&H and B&S merged into NH-BS while 12A1 and 13A1 stayed apart. Put every code that reports into an asset on that asset's row, and point a merged asset at the one that absorbed it: the registers then read as one asset without anything being renamed in SAP.
