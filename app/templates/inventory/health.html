Each count links to the coverage register its lines are shaded in — which is usually excess stock, not slow-moving: a chemical the workbook calls non-moving has little or no consumption, so its months of cover run past the excess threshold. The workbook's own "slow moving" list is its definition, not this page's {{ '{:g}'.format(thresholds.slow_moving_months) }}–{{ '{:g}'.format(thresholds.excess_stock_months) }} month band.
