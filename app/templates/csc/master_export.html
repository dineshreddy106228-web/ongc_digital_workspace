How the document is laid out

The index comes first, listing every sub-group and, under it, every specification with its page number. A sub-group header page then opens each run of sheets. Page numbers are printed as calculated and refresh to the exact printed page in Word with Ctrl+A then F9.

What is left out

Chemicals whose parameters have not been entered yet appear in the workbook but have no sheet to print, so they are not in the Word document. The issue register, impact assessment and narrative belong to the dossier — take those from Dossier Download.
